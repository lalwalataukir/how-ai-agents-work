Metrics for a compliant wealth AI bot

What to measure when an AI assistant talks to investors. The example is a wealth assistant for a mutual fund distributor: it answers fund and portfolio questions, helps start a SIP, and hands off to a relationship manager. Seven categories of metrics, each split into P0, P1 and P2 priorities.

Tap any word with a dotted underline, like , for a plain-English definition. Use the P0 / P1 / P2 buttons at the top to filter the whole page.

P0Launch blockers

Measured on every conversation from day one, with real-time alerts. A single breach is an incident. Most are zero-tolerance.

no launch without these
P1Core health

Measured from launch and reviewed weekly. They drive fixes, and release go/no-go decisions.

weekly review
P2Optimisation

Added once the bot is stable. Reviewed monthly or quarterly. They shape the roadmap and long-term trust.

monthly / quarterly

P0 launch checklist and review rhythm

Every P0 metric in one list. Before go-live, each one should be measured, alerting, and owned by a named person. Tick them off as you confirm them with your teams. Ticks are saved in this browser only.

P0 checklist

    Review rhythm

    Real timeAlerts on any P0 breach: prohibited claims, advice-boundary breaches, PII exposure, unauthorised access, successful injections, outages, cost runaway.
    WeeklyP1 review: Compliance samples transcripts, eval results for every change, escalation quality, top failure themes, latency and cost.
    MonthlyCompliance scorecard, complaint trends, fairness slices, cost per resolution, P2 trends.
    QuarterlyBoard and audit pack: model inventory, incidents, audit-trail completeness, regulatory changes and their impact.

    Questions to ask before launch

    • Which P0 metrics are automated today, and which depend on manual sampling?
    • Who gets paged for each P0 alert, and what's the response time?
    • Can we reproduce any conversation from six months ago, exactly?
    • Who signs off a prompt or model change, and where is that recorded?
    • How do we know our automated compliance checks aren't missing things?
