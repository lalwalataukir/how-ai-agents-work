Further reading

Official sources only. Tools and rules change quickly, so check dates and versions against what your team uses.

Agents in general

  • Building effective agents ↗Anthropic's engineering guide to workflows versus agents, with the common patterns. A good companion to module 1's spectrum.
  • Model Context Protocol ↗The official site for MCP: what it is, the specification, and lists of available servers. Modules 1 and 3.

The tools in modules 2 to 5

  • LangChain and LangGraph documentation ↗The official docs for both libraries. Check them against the version your team uses.
  • Langfuse documentation ↗Tracing, prompt management, evaluation and self-hosting, with current SDK names.

Safety and risk

  • OWASP GenAI Security Project ↗Home of the OWASP Top 10 for LLM applications, including prompt injection and excessive agency. Useful for module 1's Stage 4 and module 6's security metrics.
  • NIST AI Risk Management Framework ↗A widely used, vendor-neutral framework for governing AI risk. Background for module 6's governance category.

Launching safely

  • Canarying releases (Google SRE Workbook) ↗How to roll changes out to a small share of traffic first and compare. The engineering side of module 7's staged rollout.

Regulation in India (for module 6)

  • Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) ↗Circulars and regulations for intermediaries, including rules on advice and distribution.
  • Association of Mutual Funds in India (AMFI) ↗Guidelines for mutual fund distributors, and the investor education material behind standard disclosures.
  • Ministry of Electronics and IT (MeitY) ↗The Digital Personal Data Protection Act and its rules.
