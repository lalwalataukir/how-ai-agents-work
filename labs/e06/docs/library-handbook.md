# Riverside Public Library: members' handbook

This handbook covers the Main branch on Market Street and the East branch on Harbour Road. It was last updated in March 2026.

## Joining the library

Membership is free for anyone who lives, works or studies in Riverside County. Bring a photo ID and something with your address on it, such as a bill or a bank statement, to either help desk. Children under 16 need a parent or carer to sign the form. People from outside the county can join for $25 a year.

## Opening hours

The Main branch is open Monday to Friday from 9 am to 8 pm, on Saturdays from 10 am to 5 pm, and on Sundays from noon to 4 pm. The East branch keeps the same weekday and Saturday hours but is closed on Sundays. Both branches close on public holidays.

## Borrowing

You can borrow up to 12 items at a time. Books can be kept for three weeks, new releases for two weeks and DVDs for one week. You can renew an item up to three times online, in the app or at the desk, unless another member has reserved it.

## Late returns and lost items

Since 1 March 2026 there are no charges for late children's books. For adult items the charge is 20 cents a day, up to a maximum of $5 per item. If you lose an item, you pay what it costs to replace it plus a $5 processing fee. You can't borrow anything while you owe more than $10.

## E-books and audiobooks

Members can borrow e-books and audiobooks on a phone, tablet or computer with the free Libby app. Sign in with your library card number and PIN. You can have up to 10 digital loans at once, for 14 or 21 days, and they return themselves when the time is up.

## Computers, printing and Wi-Fi

Members get two hours of free computer time a day; book a computer at the desk or online. Printing costs 10 cents a page in black and white and 50 cents a page in colour. The free Wi-Fi network is called Riverside-Guest and has no password.

## Meeting rooms

Members can book a meeting room for free for non-commercial use, up to two weeks ahead and for at most three hours at a time. Book online or at the Main branch desk. Rooms for paid events cost $15 an hour.

## The AI research helper

Until September 2026 the library is trialling an AI assistant that helps you find sources for school and work projects. It answers using only the library's catalogue and licensed databases, and every answer links to the books or articles it used. Librarians review a sample of its answers every week.

## Accessibility

Both branches have step-free access and accessible toilets. We lend large-print books and audiobooks on CD, and a home delivery service brings books to members who can't visit because of illness or disability.

## Contact us

Call the help desk on 555-0142 or email help@riverside-library.example. Staff answer emails within two working days.
